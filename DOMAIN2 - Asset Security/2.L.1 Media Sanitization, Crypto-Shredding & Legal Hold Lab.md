# 2.L.1 Media Sanitization, Crypto-Shredding & Legal Hold Lab

> ℹ **Lab Objective:** Practice applying NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2 sanitization standards across mixed storage hardware and resolving conflicts between retention schedules and legal holds.
### Scenario Context ###

**GlobalLogix Corp** is decommissioning an on-premises server room and migrating workloads to a multi-tenant public cloud. At 09:30 AM, General Counsel notifies the CISO of an impending regulatory investigation involving freight contractor invoices from 2024–2025.

### Task 1: NIST SP 800-88 Sanitization Decision Matrix ###

Select the **most compliant AND cost-effective** sanitization category (*Clear*, *Purge*, or *Destroy*) for each asset below:

| Asset & Sensitivity | Lifecycle Disposition | Chosen Method | Why Alternative Methods Fail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A.** 40 Magnetic HDDs storing *Internal* warehouse schedules | Redeploy to another internal branch | *Fill in* | *Fill in* |
| **B.** 25 NVMe SSDs storing *Restricted* customer KYC records | Return to hardware leasing vendor | *Fill in* | *Fill in* |
| **C.** 12 LTO-8 Backup Tapes storing *Confidential* M&A plans | End-of-life permanent disposal | *Fill in* | *Fill in* |
| **D.** Multi-tenant Cloud S3 Buckets storing *Restricted* medical claims | Terminate cloud provider contract | *Fill in* | *Fill in* |
| **E.** 3 Damaged SSDs storing *Restricted* signing keys (controller fried) | End-of-life permanent disposal | *Fill in* | *Fill in* |
### Solution Key (Task 1)

- **A (Internal Magnetic HDDs, Internal Reuse):** **Clear** (single-pass overwrite / Zero Fill). Sufficient for low-sensitivity internal redeployment while keeping functional drives usable.
- **B (Restricted NVMe SSDs, Vendor Return):** **Purge — Cryptographic Erase (CE)** or vendor-supported **NVMe Sanitize / Block Erase**. *Why:* Standard file-level overwriting misses SSD wear-leveling spare blocks, and degaussing has zero effect on flash memory.
- **C (Confidential LTO-8 Tapes, Disposal):** **Purge (High-Coercivity Degaussing)** followed by **Destroy (Cross-cut Shredding / Incineration)**. Degaussing disrupts magnetic domains and physical destruction eliminates any residual risk.
- **D (Cloud S3 Buckets, Contract Termination):** **Purge — Crypto-Shredding** (revoke and permanently destroy the Customer-Managed Keys in KMS/HSM). *Why:* Customers have no physical access to shred or degauss a cloud provider's multi-tenant storage arrays.
- **E (Damaged SSDs with Dead Controller):** **Destroy — Disintegration / Pulverization / Incineration** (particle size $\le 2\text{ mm}$). *Why:* Because the controller hardware is dead, electronic Purge/Crypto-Erase commands cannot execute on the NAND flash chips.

### Task 2: Retention Schedule vs. Legal Hold Conflict ###

At 10:00 AM, your automated lifecycle script warns that **600 GB of 2024 freight contractor emails and invoices** are scheduled for automatic crypto-shredding at midnight because they just reached their 2-year retention expiration.

Answer these three CISO governance questions:
1. **What action must you take before midnight, and what legal risk occurs if the script runs?**
2. **Should you freeze deletion across the entire company's email system or apply a scoped hold?**
3. **When can normal retention deletion resume?**
### Solution Key (Task 2)

1. **Immediate Action:** Issue a formal **Legal Hold (Litigation Hold)** and suspend the midnight deletion job immediately. Allowing scheduled deletion after receiving notice of an investigation constitutes **spoliation of evidence**, exposing the organization to severe court sanctions and adverse inference rulings.
2. **Scope:** Apply a **targeted, defensible scope** (specific custodians, contractor domains, and date ranges defined with General Counsel) rather than freezing deletion enterprise-wide, which would violate data minimization and inflate privacy/breach liability.
3. **Resumption:** Only after General Counsel issues a written **Legal Hold Release** confirming the investigation or litigation has formally concluded.
